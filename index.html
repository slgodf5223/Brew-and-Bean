Our Story

Started by two friends who love great coffee and better conversation, Brew-and-Bean is a neighborhood cafe dedicated to simple, delicious drinks and a warm atmosphere. We roast our beans in small batches and source thoughtfully from trusted farmers.

Come for the coffee, stay for the community — we host weekly events, local music, and pop-up pastry nights.
